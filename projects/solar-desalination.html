Tools

Thermal system design and financial model · 2024

A 500,000 liter-per-day desalination plant driven by a concentrated solar power tower with eight hours of molten salt thermal storage. I ran the thermal calculations across the solar field, the storage block and the MED train, then built the supporting case: $27M CAPEX, a 10 to 12 year break-even and a 30-year design life, sizing the plant against both thermal performance and project economics.

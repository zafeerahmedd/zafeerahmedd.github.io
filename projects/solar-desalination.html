06 / THERMAL SYSTEMS

Solar desalination

CSP with multi-effect distillation: a thermal design and financial modelling study.

Thermal system design and financial model · 2024

A 500,000 litre-per-day desalination plant driven by a concentrated solar power tower with eight hours of molten salt thermal storage. I ran the thermal calculations across the solar field, the storage block and the MED train, then built the supporting case: $27M CAPEX, a 10 to 12 year break-even and a 30-year design life, sizing the plant against both thermal performance and project economics.

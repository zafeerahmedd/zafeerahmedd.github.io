02BUILD AND TEST

02 / Build and test

Test. Fail. Understand.

A tracked belt-drive platform taken past its working limit rather than stopped at the last success. Four stair rises were recorded with speed and power draw at each, and the statics were worked to explain the failure. The answer was traction and stability at 45 degrees, not torque: 2.06 N·m required against 5 N·m supplied.

Motion, printed.

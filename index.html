02BUILD AND TEST

02 / Build and Test

Stair-Climbing Robot

Test. Fail. Understand.

A tracked belt-drive platform taken past its working limit rather than stopped at the last success. Four stair rises were recorded with speed and power draw at each, and the statics were worked to explain the failure. Required torque falls as the stairs steepen, from 2.71 N·m at 22° to 2.06 N·m at 45°, against 5 N·m supplied by the four motors combined. The ceiling was never power: it was traction and stability at 45°.

03SIMULATION

03 / Simulation

CFD & Heat-Transfer Studies

Trust, then verify.

Five fluid and heat-transfer problems solved end to end in SOLIDWORKS Flow Simulation, each checked by hand against closed-form theory or a published standard rather than accepted as found. Seven independent validations across the five studies, four of them inside 1.2%: the venturi recovered a discharge coefficient of 0.983 against the ISO 5167-4 reference of 0.995, and an LMTD built as a custom solver goal was verified by hand to 0.007%.
